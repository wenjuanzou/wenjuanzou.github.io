About

I'm Wenjuan, and most people call me Joerica.

I design learning for the moment someone gets stuck: a new rep on a discovery call, a client setting up their first study at 10 p.m. in another time zone, a nurse in a hard conversation with a patient. I start from what that person needs to do differently, then build the practice, the job aid or the workflow that gets them there.

I came to learning design through interaction design. My M.S. at Northeastern taught me to think about the interface first, and that habit never left. I built clinical eLearning for 350+ nurses at Commonwealth Care Alliance, rebuilt a university Title IX course that raised completion by 20%, and spent years at TransPerfect localizing and testing enterprise courses across Western European and East Asian languages. That is where I learned how courses actually break.

In 2023 I went back to the science with Harvard Extension's Learning Design and Technology program. Then I joined Satellica, an AI-powered UX research platform, where I ran client education and research operations: self-serve product guides that cut routine how-to questions by about 80%, and human-in-the-loop Claude workflows that saved about 40% of my weekly coordination time.

I'm based in Greater Boston and work in English and Mandarin.

Methods

How I choose a process

ADDIE

For well-defined, end-to-end builds where audience analysis, objectives and evaluation need to be planned up front.

SAM

For tight timelines or shifting SME input: quick prototypes in Storyline, reviewed early and often.

Double Diamond

For behavior-change problems where the real need isn't clear yet: understand learners first, then design and test.

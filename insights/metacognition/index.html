How do we describe metacognition?

February 2025, by Wenjuan (Joerica) Zou

Metacognition is "thinking about thinking." It's the learner's ability to be aware of their own thought processes and regulate them. In learning design, this means helping learners pause, reflect and adapt their approach as they go. It's not just about learning content, but learning how to learn better.

Three dimensions of metacognition

Person. Self-awareness: recognizing your own habits, strengths and emotions as a learner. As designers, we can ask learners to think back to a recent time they struggled with a task. Was it distractions, unclear instructions or lack of confidence? These reflections help learners understand their tendencies and set the stage for growth.

Task. The nature and complexity of the task itself. Imagine a branching scenario in healthcare training: before learners choose how to communicate with a patient, they need to analyze a chart and interpret the situation. That pause to assess the task helps them slow down, prioritize and make more informed choices, just as they would on the job.

Strategy. Stepping back to ask whether the methods you're using are working. In a microlearning module, learners might be offered several paths to solve a problem and prompted along the way: "Why did you choose this path?" and "What would you try next time?" Over time, they take ownership by adjusting strategies with intention.

Why it matters

Metacognitive strategies lead to deeper learning and better retention. They build self-regulated learners who can plan, monitor and evaluate their own progress, which matters most for busy adults learning on their own.

How I build it into my designs

  • Reflective prompts before and after activities: "What do I already know?" and later, "What would I do differently?"
  • Feedback loops that go beyond right or wrong, asking learners to explain why an answer is correct.
  • Choice and strategy use: optional paths and resources so learners can decide what they need based on their confidence.

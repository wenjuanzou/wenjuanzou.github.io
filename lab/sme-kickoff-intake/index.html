How this intake works

Before a training kickoff, the subject matter expert has a short, guided conversation with an AI interviewer. The designer gets a draft brief, and the kickoff meeting goes to decisions instead of fact-finding.

Experts are treated as expertsThe interviewer asks about their cases and judgment, never about who is at fault, and checks back after each part that it heard them right.
Built for busy, interrupted daysEvery answer saves. Pause anytime; on return the interviewer recaps where you were and asks the last question again.
AI drafts, people decideThe SME reviews and corrects a plain-language summary before it is sent. The designer confirms every item of the brief at kickoff.

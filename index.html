How I work

Four habits behind every project

Start with the job

I find the task people are stuck on and what getting it wrong costs before I decide what to build.

Practice over content

Learners make realistic decisions and get feedback that explains the tradeoff, not just right or wrong.

Support the moment of need

Short clips, job aids and self-serve guides do more on the job than a course people finish once.

AI with a human at the gate

I use AI to draft, build and automate, and I review every output before it reaches a learner.

About

A designer who learned how courses break

I trained in interaction design, built clinical and university eLearning, then spent years at TransPerfect making enterprise courses work across languages and systems. After a graduate certificate in Learning Design and Technology at Harvard Extension, I joined an AI research startup to run client education and research operations.

That mix is why I design the way I do: I think about the interface, the learner and the system the course has to live in.

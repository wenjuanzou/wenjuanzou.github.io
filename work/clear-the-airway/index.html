01. The problem

Hesitation costs time in a pediatric emergency

Early-career clinicians often lack hands-on exposure to every piece of airway equipment. In an emergency, hesitation over sizing or setup delays care. I took Dr. Traci Wolbrink's OPENPediatrics video on pediatric intubation and turned the equipment-preparation portion into an interactive primer.

02. Goals

What learners practice

  • Identify and select the right airway equipment.
  • Build spatial familiarity with where equipment sits in a patient room.
  • Get feedback on sizing and usage choices.

03. Key decisions

From watching to doing

1

Explore a 360° room

Learners search a 360° patient room to find each tool before answering questions about it.

Why: recognition in context is closer to the real task than a list of names.

2

Short checks right after each clip

Knowledge checks sit next to the video segment they test, so feedback arrives while the content is fresh.

3

A completion certificate

A micro-certificate closes the module so learners have a record of what they practiced.

04. What I'd do next

Cover the whole procedure

Expand into a full branching simulation covering the procedure and post-intubation care.

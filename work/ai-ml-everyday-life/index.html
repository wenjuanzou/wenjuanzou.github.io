01. The brief

Explain AI vs. ML to people who use it every day

This module was my response to a full-stack instructional design test. The brief: introduce non-technical learners to the basics of artificial intelligence and machine learning, the difference between them, and their impact on daily life, in five days.

02. Learning objectives

What learners can do afterward

  • Explain AI vs. ML in simple, clear language.
  • Identify common AI- and ML-powered features in everyday digital experiences.
  • Recognize both the benefits and the concerns of smart technologies.

03. Key decisions

Abstract concepts, familiar screens

1

One character, one app

Learners follow Alex through a banking app: a fraud alert, a personalized credit offer and a virtual assistant. Each feature introduces one concept.

Why: anchoring abstract ideas in a familiar product makes them easier to explain to a customer later.

2

Design the app, not just the slides

I mapped realistic user interactions in Google Flow and built mobile banking UI mockups in Canva, so the scenario looks like the products learners support.

3

Use AI to move fast, keep judgment human

Perplexity for research and definitions, HeyGen for an avatar-led introduction. I checked content accuracy and wrote the knowledge checks myself.

04. Reflection

Clarity under a deadline

Five days forced choices: one narrative, three features, short checks. It sharpened how I distill a technical topic and use AI tools across the whole process, from research to UI visuals.
